Custom Pallet Order Process: How to Specify Load Ratings, Dimensions, and Material Requirements

Heavydutypallets Editorial • September 2026

Understanding Custom Pallet Specifications

When standard pallet dimensions and load capacities don't align with your material handling requirements, custom pallets become essential. For procurement teams managing specialized logistics operations—pharmaceutical cold chain, automotive parts distribution, or bulk food ingredient handling—the custom order process requires precise specification of structural requirements, material certifications, and performance parameters.

Unlike commodity wood pallets ordered by the truckload, custom heavy duty PP pallets from Elipacko are engineered to exact operational tolerances. The specification process determines not only physical dimensions but also deck configuration, runner spacing, fork entry angles, and static versus dynamic load performance.

Load Rating Requirements: Static, Dynamic, and Racking Loads

Every custom pallet specification begins with load analysis. Three load ratings govern pallet structural design:

  • Static load capacity: Maximum weight the pallet can support when stationary on a flat surface, typically 4–6× the dynamic rating
  • Dynamic load capacity: Safe working load during transport and handling with forklifts or pallet jacks, usually 2,000–5,000 lbs for industrial PP pallets
  • Racking load capacity: Weight supported when loaded onto selective pallet racking, often 50–70% of static capacity due to cantilever stress

Specify each rating independently. A pallet rated for 5,000 lbs dynamic load and 10,000 lbs static may only support 6,000 lbs when racked, depending on deck deflection limits and runner span. If your operation includes both floor stacking and rack storage, the racking load becomes the constraining design parameter.

Dimensional and Structural Specifications

Custom pallet dimensions must account for container utilization, rack bay widths, and automated handling equipment clearances. Standard pallet footprints (48×40, 42×42, 48×48) optimize for North American logistics, but custom operations may require Euro pallets (1200×800 mm), half-pallets for pharmaceutical cleanrooms, or oversized platforms for bulk totes.

Structural specifications include:

  • Deck configuration: Solid top deck for small parts, slatted deck for airflow in cold storage, perforated deck for drainage in wet environments
  • Runner height and spacing: Standard 5-inch runner height for manual pallet jacks; 6–7 inches for automated guided vehicles (AGVs) with varying ground clearance
  • Entry configuration: Four-way entry for maximum flexibility, two-way entry for cost optimization in unidirectional flow operations
  • Reinforcement ribs: Additional crossbeams or gussets for extreme load concentration or impact resistance in rough handling environments

Elipacko manufactures custom pallets with dimensional tolerances of ±3 mm, critical for automated storage and retrieval systems (AS/RS) where pallet misalignment causes jam conditions.

Material Certifications and Compliance Requirements

For food, pharmaceutical, and export applications, material certifications drive pallet specification. FDA-compliant virgin polypropylene resin is required for direct food contact applications, while recycled-content PP may suffice for non-food industrial use.

Specify certifications at the order stage:

  • FDA 21 CFR 177.1520: Virgin PP resin for food contact
  • USDA accepted: Non-porous plastic pallets for meat and poultry facilities
  • ISPM-15 exempt: PP pallets require no heat treatment for international shipping, reducing export documentation and fumigation costs
  • ISO 8611: Dimensional tolerances for pallets used in international logistics

Elipacko sources FDA-compliant resin and provides material certificates with each shipment. For pharmaceutical distribution centers requiring full traceability, batch-level resin documentation is available upon request.

Lead Times and Order Quantities

Custom pallet lead times vary by tooling requirements. If your specification matches an existing mold geometry with dimensional adjustments (runner spacing, deck perforation pattern), lead time is typically 4–6 weeks for ocean freight from Thailand or Vietnam manufacturing facilities. New tooling for fully custom designs adds 8–12 weeks for mold fabrication and first-article approval.

Minimum order quantities for custom pallets range from 500–1,000 units depending on size and complexity. Higher volumes (5,000+ units) enable cost optimization through dedicated production runs and consolidated ocean freight. For operations transitioning from wood to plastic pallets, phased rollouts allow validation of custom specifications before full fleet conversion.

Elipacko's US manufacturing facility currently in development will reduce lead times to 2–3 weeks for North American customers, enabling just-in-time replenishment for closed-loop pallet pool operations.

Specification Validation and Sampling

Before committing to full production, request first-article samples for validation testing. Samples allow verification of dimensional accuracy, load testing under actual operational conditions, and compatibility with existing material handling equipment.

Validation testing should include:

  • Load testing: Static load test to 1.5× rated capacity for 24 hours, dynamic load test through simulated handling cycles
  • Dimensional inspection: Caliper measurements of critical dimensions, flatness measurement with dial indicator
  • Equipment compatibility: Trial runs with forklifts, pallet jacks, conveyor systems, and automated stretch wrappers
  • Environmental testing: Cold storage performance at -20°F, heat deflection testing for summer warehouse conditions

Specification adjustments identified during sampling—such as increased rib thickness for deflection control or modified runner geometry for conveyor tracking—can be incorporated before full production tooling is finalized.

For procurement teams specifying custom heavy duty pallets, the order process balances engineering precision with supply chain practicality. Clear specification of load ratings, dimensional requirements, and material certifications ensures Elipacko industrial packaging delivers pallets that perform reliably across your entire logistics network.
