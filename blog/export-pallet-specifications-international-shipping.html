Export Pallet Specifications for International Shipping: What Buyers Need to Know

Heavydutypallets Editorial • September 2026

Why Export Pallet Specifications Matter for Cross-Border Shipments

When shipping goods internationally, pallet choice directly impacts customs clearance speed, container efficiency, and total landed cost. Wood pallets require ISPM-15 heat treatment and certification, adding 3–5 days to lead times and $8–$15 per pallet in treatment fees. Plastic pallets, particularly heavy-duty PP models, bypass these requirements entirely while offering consistent dimensions and superior durability across multiple trips.

Procurement teams managing international supply chains need pallets that meet weight restrictions, fit standard container widths, and withstand humidity changes during ocean freight. The right specification reduces damage claims, eliminates quarantine delays, and cuts per-trip costs when pallets return through closed-loop networks.

Standard Export Pallet Dimensions and Container Optimization

The two dominant export pallet footprints are 48×40 inches (1219×1016 mm) for North American trade and 1200×1000 mm for European and Asian routes. A 40-foot high-cube container fits twenty 48×40 pallets in a single layer or twenty-one 1200×1000 pallets, making dimensional accuracy critical for maximizing cube utilization.

Heavy-duty plastic pallets from Elipacko are manufactured in both standard footprints with tolerance held to ±3 mm, ensuring consistent fit across container types. Custom sizes are available for non-standard cargo, with lead times of 4–6 weeks for tooling and first production run. When ordering custom export pallets, specify fork entry on all four sides and top deck grid spacing that accommodates your carton or bag dimensions.

Load Ratings and Structural Requirements for Ocean Freight

Export pallets endure dynamic loads during truck transport, static loads during warehousing, and racking loads in distribution centers. A typical export pallet specification requires 2,500 lbs dynamic load capacity, 5,000 lbs static load, and 2,000 lbs per pair of rack beams when used in selective racking.

PP pallets achieve these ratings through reinforced rib geometry and material gauge. Twin-sheet thermoformed pallets use 4–6 mm wall thickness; injection-molded structural pallets use ribbed designs with localized thickness up to 12 mm at stress points. Heavy duty PP pallets from Elipacko are tested to ASME MH1 and ISO 8611 standards, with certified load ratings documented on the spec sheet provided with each order.

When specifying load ratings, distinguish between uniformly distributed loads and concentrated point loads. A pallet rated for 5,000 lbs static load may fail under a 1,200 lb concentrated load if the top deck lacks sufficient grid density. For heavy machinery parts or coil stock, specify a minimum of nine top deck supports or request a custom deck layout.

Material Specifications: Virgin PP, Regrind Content, and FDA Compliance

Export pallets carrying food, pharmaceutical, or cosmetic products require virgin polypropylene resin that meets FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 for food contact. Pallets made from post-consumer regrind are suitable for automotive, electronics, and industrial applications but cannot be used in food supply chains without certification.

Elipacko industrial packaging uses FDA-compliant virgin PP copolymer for all food-grade pallets, with full traceability to resin lot and production date. The material offers impact resistance down to -20°C, UV stabilization for outdoor storage, and chemical resistance to cleaning agents including quaternary ammonium compounds and hydrogen peroxide.

Specify material grade when ordering: copolymer PP for cold-chain applications, homopolymer PP for high-temperature environments, and UV-stabilized formulations for pallets stored outdoors or in open-sided warehouses. Color options include natural gray, blue, black, and custom Pantone matches for brand identification in pooled systems.

Rackable vs. Nestable Pallets for Export Applications

Rackable pallets have solid top decks and bottom runner profiles that sit flush on rack beams, supporting high static loads but requiring full cubic volume during return shipment. Nestable pallets have perimeter runners and sloped profiles that allow 6–8 pallets to nest in the height of two standard pallets, reducing return freight costs by 70% but limiting racking suitability.

For closed-loop export routes with guaranteed return loads, rackable pallets maximize warehouse efficiency. For one-way export with occasional returns or consolidation programs, nestable pallets cut return freight cost from $22 per pallet to $6 per pallet on LTL shipments. Evaluate total cost per trip, including both outbound and return legs, when choosing between designs.

Custom Features for Specialty Export Applications

Standard export pallets serve 80% of use cases, but specialty cargo often requires custom features. Options include perimeter lips to retain small parts, anti-slip top surfaces for bagged goods, RFID pockets for tracking in pooled systems, and steel reinforcement bars for extreme load applications. Lead time for custom tooling is 6–8 weeks, with minimum order quantities of 500–1,000 pallets depending on complexity.

When specifying custom features, provide CAD drawings of the cargo footprint, weight distribution diagram, and handling equipment details (fork width, clamp dimensions). Elipacko engineering teams review custom specs within 48 hours and provide DFM feedback to optimize manufacturability and cost.
